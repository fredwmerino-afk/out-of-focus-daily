Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

This policy explains what information Out of Focus Daily (“the game”, “we”), available at out-of-focus-games.com, collects and how it is used. The short version: you can play without an account, we don’t ask for your name or email, and your game stats stay in your own browser.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on today’s puzzle and your stats (games played, current streak and best streak) in your browser’s local storage. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.

So the game opens quickly and works offline, your browser also keeps copies of the game’s pages and painting images. These are the same files anyone can download from the site, and they contain no personal information.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider, Netlify, receives standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. It uses this to deliver and secure the site and may keep it in server logs. We may also use Netlify’s aggregate traffic statistics, which count visits without using cookies.

The game’s fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser also connects to Google’s servers. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Advertising

We may show ads served by Google AdSense to help cover the cost of running the game.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Where the law requires it, for example for visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, we will ask for your consent before cookies are used for advertising.

If you contact us

If you send a message through our contact form, we receive what you type, including any email address you choose to give us. We use it only to read and reply to your message. Form messages are stored by Netlify, our hosting provider. We don’t sell this information or add you to a mailing list.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Clear your browser’s site data to remove your saved game progress and stats.
  • Use your browser settings to block or delete cookies.
  • Use the opt-out links above to limit personalized ads.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
