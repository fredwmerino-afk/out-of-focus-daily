Rembrandt van Rijn

Dutch, 1606–1669

Rembrandt was the greatest painter of the Dutch Golden Age, celebrated for his group portraits, Bible scenes and dramatic use of light and shadow. His many self-portraits trace his life from confident young success to bankruptcy and old age.
