Vincent van Gogh

Dutch, 1853–1890

Van Gogh painted for only about ten years, producing more than 800 paintings, yet sold very few in his lifetime. His thick, swirling brushwork and intense color, developed in Paris and the south of France, made him one of the most influential artists of all time.
