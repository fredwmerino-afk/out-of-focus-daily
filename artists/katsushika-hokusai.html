Katsushika Hokusai

Japanese, 1760–1849

Hokusai made thousands of prints, paintings and drawings over a long career, using dozens of names. His series Thirty-Six Views of Mount Fuji, which includes The Great Wave, influenced Monet, Van Gogh and many other Western artists.
