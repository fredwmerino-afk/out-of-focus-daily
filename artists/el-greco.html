El Greco

Greek, active in Spain, 1541–1614

Born in Crete, El Greco (“the Greek”) trained in Venice and Rome before settling in Toledo, Spain. His elongated figures and flickering colors looked strange to many of his contemporaries but inspired modern artists centuries later.
