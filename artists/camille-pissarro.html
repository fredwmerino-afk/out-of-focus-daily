Camille Pissarro

French, born Danish West Indies, 1830–1903

Pissarro was the only artist to show work at all eight Impressionist exhibitions, and a generous mentor to younger painters, including Cézanne and Gauguin. He loved painting rural life and, later, the busy streets of Paris.
