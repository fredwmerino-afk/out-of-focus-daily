Utagawa Hiroshige

Japanese, 1797–1858

Hiroshige was the great rival of Hokusai and the master of the travel print. His series Fifty-three Stations of the Tōkaidō and One Hundred Famous Views of Edo capture rain, snow, mist and moonlight, and Van Gogh copied two of his prints in oil.
