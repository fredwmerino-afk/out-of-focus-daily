About Out of Focus

A free daily puzzle for anyone who loves paintings, or would like to.

Out of Focus Daily turns great paintings into a guessing game. Each one starts as a blurry mosaic and sharpens step by step, and the challenge is to recognize it from its colors and shapes alone. It’s a quick daily habit, and a surprisingly good way to learn to recognize artists and styles.

Out of Focus Games is a small independent project made by Freddy M., an art history lover based near Kansas City. There are no accounts and nothing to install: just open the page and play.

About the paintings

Every image in the game is a public-domain work: the artists died long ago and their paintings are free for anyone to use. The images come from the open-access programs of museums that share their collections with the public, including:

  • The Art Institute of Chicago
  • The National Gallery of Art, Washington
  • The Mauritshuis, The Hague
  • Wikimedia Commons, for photographs of works in other museums such as the Louvre, the Prado and the Uffizi

The label after each painting gives the museum where it hangs today, so you can go and see the real thing. The short facts on each label were written for the game from museum sources.

Spotted a mistake?
