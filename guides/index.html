Art guides

Every painting in Out of Focus, with the story behind it, and short guides to the great periods of Western art.

Out of Focus is a daily art puzzle: each painting starts as a blurry mosaic and sharpens step by step until you can name it. These pages tell you about the 200+ paintings in the game: who painted them, when, where you can see them today and why they matter. Read up before you play, or come back after a round to learn about the painting that stumped you.
