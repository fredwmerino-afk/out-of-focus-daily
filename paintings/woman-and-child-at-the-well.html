About this painting

Camille Pissarro made Woman and Child at the Well in 1882. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Pissarro was the only artist to show work at all eight Impressionist exhibitions.

About the artist

Pissarro was the only artist to show work at all eight Impressionist exhibitions, and a generous mentor to younger painters, including Cézanne and Gauguin. He loved painting rural life and, later, the busy streets of Paris. More about Camille Pissarro
