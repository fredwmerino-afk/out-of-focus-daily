About this painting

Willem Claesz Heda made Banquet Piece with Mince Pie in 1635. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the National Gallery of Art, Washington.

A half-peeled lemon and a toppled cup suggest a meal just abandoned. Quiet, silvery still lifes like this were a Haarlem specialty.
