About this painting

Caspar David Friedrich made Wanderer above the Sea of Fog around 1818. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the Hamburger Kunsthalle, Hamburg.

We see the man only from behind, a device Friedrich used often so that viewers share the figure’s view.

About the artist

Friedrich was the leading painter of German Romanticism. His lonely figures, often seen from behind, gaze out at mountains, seas and moonlit skies, inviting viewers to share their sense of awe. More about Caspar David Friedrich
