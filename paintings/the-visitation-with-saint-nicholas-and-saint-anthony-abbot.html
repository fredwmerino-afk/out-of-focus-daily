About this painting

Piero di Cosimo made The Visitation with Saint Nicholas and Saint Anthony Abbot around 1489–90. It is oil on panel, and you can see it today at the National Gallery of Art, Washington.

The Capponi family commissioned it for their chapel in the church of Santo Spirito in Florence. Piero was about 27 when he painted it.
