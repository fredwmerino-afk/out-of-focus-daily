About this print

Kitagawa Utamaro made Young Woman Blowing a Popen around 1792–93. It is color woodblock print, and you can see it today at The Metropolitan Museum of Art.

A popen, or poppin, is a glass toy with a thin bottom that clicks in and out when you blow into it. The series claimed to reveal women’s characters from their faces.

About the artist

Utamaro was the leading designer of prints of beautiful women in the 1790s, famous for his close-up “large head” portraits set against shimmering mica backgrounds. More about Kitagawa Utamaro
