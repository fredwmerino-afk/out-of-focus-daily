About this painting

Rogier van der Weyden made Portrait of Jean Gros around 1460–64. It is oil on panel, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Originally one half of a hinged pair: Jean Gros, a Burgundian court official, prayed toward a separate panel of the Virgin and Child. His coat of arms is painted on the back.
