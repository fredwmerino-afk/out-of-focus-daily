About this painting

Maurice Prendergast made The Terrace Bridge, Central Park in 1901. It is watercolor, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Prendergast is one of the few Americans counted among the Post-Impressionists. Bethesda Terrace in Central Park still looks much like this.
