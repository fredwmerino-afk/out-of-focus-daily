About this painting

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec made Confetti in 1894. It is color lithograph (poster), and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

An advertising poster for a London maker of paper confetti.

About the artist

Toulouse-Lautrec, from an aristocratic family, made the cabarets, dance halls and theaters of Montmartre his subject. His bold posters for the Moulin Rouge helped turn advertising into an art form. More about Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec
