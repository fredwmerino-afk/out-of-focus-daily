About this painting

Claude Monet made Stacks of Wheat (Sunset, Snow Effect) in 1890–91. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Monet painted about 25 canvases of the wheat stacks near his home in Giverny, each in different light, weather or season.

About the artist

Monet was a founder of Impressionism; one of his paintings, Impression, Sunrise, gave the movement its name. He painted outdoors to capture changing light, and in later life returned again and again to the same subjects, from haystacks to the water lily pond in his garden at Giverny.
