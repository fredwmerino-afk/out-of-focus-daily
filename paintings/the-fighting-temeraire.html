About this painting

J. M. W. Turner made The Fighting Temeraire in 1839. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the National Gallery, London.

A ghostly old warship that fought at Trafalgar is towed by a smoky steam tug to be broken up for scrap. It appears on the Bank of England’s £20 note.

About the artist

Turner was the most daring British painter of his day, dissolving storms, seas and sunsets into swirling light and color. He left most of his work to the British nation, and Britain’s top contemporary art award, the Turner Prize, is named after him. More about J. M. W. Turner
