About this painting

Pierre-Auguste Renoir made Young Woman Sewing in 1879. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

As a teenager Renoir worked in a porcelain factory painting flowers on plates, and his love of flowers stayed with him.

About the artist

Renoir was a leading Impressionist, known for warm, glowing scenes of people enjoying themselves: dances, boating parties, cafés and family life. Late in life, despite severe arthritis, he kept on painting. More about Pierre-Auguste Renoir
