About this painting

Caspar David Friedrich made Chalk Cliffs on Rügen around 1818. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the Museum Oskar Reinhart, Winterthur.

Probably painted after Friedrich’s honeymoon on the Baltic island of Rügen. The three figures may be the artist, his wife and his brother.

About the artist

Friedrich was the leading painter of German Romanticism. His lonely figures, often seen from behind, gaze out at mountains, seas and moonlit skies, inviting viewers to share their sense of awe. More about Caspar David Friedrich
