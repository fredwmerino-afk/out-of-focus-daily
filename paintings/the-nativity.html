About this painting

Fra Bartolommeo made The Nativity in 1504–07. It is oil on panel, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Fra Bartolommeo gave up painting for several years after becoming a Dominican friar. He made this soon after returning to his art in 1504.
