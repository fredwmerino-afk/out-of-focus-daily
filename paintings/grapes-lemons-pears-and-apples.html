About this painting

Vincent van Gogh made Grapes, Lemons, Pears, and Apples in 1887. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Van Gogh painted a series of fruit still lifes in Paris to practice the bright colors he was discovering there.

About the artist

Van Gogh painted for only about ten years, producing more than 800 paintings, yet sold very few in his lifetime. His thick, swirling brushwork and intense color, developed in Paris and the south of France, made him one of the most influential artists of all time. More about Vincent van Gogh
