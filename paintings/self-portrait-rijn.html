About this painting

Rembrandt van Rijn made Self-Portrait (Rembrandt, 1659) in 1659. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the National Gallery of Art, Washington.

Rembrandt painted about 40 self-portraits. This one was made soon after he went bankrupt.

About the artist

Rembrandt was the greatest painter of the Dutch Golden Age, celebrated for his group portraits, Bible scenes and dramatic use of light and shadow. His many self-portraits trace his life from confident young success to bankruptcy and old age. More about Rembrandt van Rijn
