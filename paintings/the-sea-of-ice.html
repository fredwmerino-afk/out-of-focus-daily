About this painting

Caspar David Friedrich made The Sea of Ice in 1823–24. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the Hamburger Kunsthalle, Hamburg.

Look closely at the right: the stern of a crushed ship is disappearing under the slabs of ice. The painting never sold in Friedrich’s lifetime.

About the artist

Friedrich was the leading painter of German Romanticism. His lonely figures, often seen from behind, gaze out at mountains, seas and moonlit skies, inviting viewers to share their sense of awe. More about Caspar David Friedrich
