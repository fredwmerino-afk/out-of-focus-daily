About this painting

Odilon Redon made Still Life with Flowers (Redon) in 1905. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Redon spent his early career making dark, dreamlike charcoal drawings, then turned late in life to glowing flowers like these.
