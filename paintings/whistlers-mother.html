About this painting

James McNeill Whistler made Whistler’s Mother (Arrangement in Grey and Black No. 1) in 1871. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the Musée d’Orsay, Paris.

Whistler called it Arrangement in Grey and Black No. 1 and insisted it was about color and form. The public named it after the sitter, his mother Anna.

About the artist

Whistler was an American painter who worked in London and Paris. He gave his paintings musical titles like Arrangement and Nocturne, insisting they were about color and harmony rather than stories. More about James McNeill Whistler
