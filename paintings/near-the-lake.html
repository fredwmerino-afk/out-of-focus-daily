About this painting

Pierre-Auguste Renoir made Near the Lake around 1879. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Renoir loved painting Parisians at leisure outdoors, with sunlight broken up by leaves and water.

About the artist

Renoir was a leading Impressionist, known for warm, glowing scenes of people enjoying themselves: dances, boating parties, cafés and family life. Late in life, despite severe arthritis, he kept on painting. More about Pierre-Auguste Renoir
