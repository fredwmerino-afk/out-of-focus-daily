About this painting

Pierre-Auguste Renoir made Woman at the Piano in 1875–76. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Renoir returned to piano players often, most famously in Young Girls at the Piano (1892).

About the artist

Renoir was a leading Impressionist, known for warm, glowing scenes of people enjoying themselves: dances, boating parties, cafés and family life. Late in life, despite severe arthritis, he kept on painting. More about Pierre-Auguste Renoir
