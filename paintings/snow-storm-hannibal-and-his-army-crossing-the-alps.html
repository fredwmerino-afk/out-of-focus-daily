About this painting

J. M. W. Turner made Snow Storm: Hannibal and his Army Crossing the Alps in 1812. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at Tate Britain, London.

Turner based the storm on one he watched over the Yorkshire hills. Hannibal himself is almost lost, a tiny figure on an elephant in the distance.

About the artist

Turner was the most daring British painter of his day, dissolving storms, seas and sunsets into swirling light and color. He left most of his work to the British nation, and Britain’s top contemporary art award, the Turner Prize, is named after him. More about J. M. W. Turner
