About this painting

Théodore Géricault made The Raft of the Medusa in 1818–19. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the Musée du Louvre, Paris.

Based on a real 1816 shipwreck: of about 150 people set adrift on a raft, only 15 survived. Géricault interviewed survivors and built a model of the raft.

About the artist

Géricault was a pioneer of French Romanticism, passionate about horses and dramatic modern subjects. He died at 32, but his Raft of the Medusa changed the course of French painting. More about Théodore Géricault
