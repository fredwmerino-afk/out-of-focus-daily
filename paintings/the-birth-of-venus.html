About this painting

Sandro Botticelli made The Birth of Venus around 1484–86. It is tempera on canvas, and you can see it today at the Uffizi Galleries, Florence.

A pagan goddess on a huge scale was a bold choice in Florence, where big paintings were almost always religious.

About the artist

Botticelli was a favorite painter of Florence’s Medici family. He is best known for graceful mythological paintings like The Birth of Venus, as well as many tender Madonnas. More about Sandro Botticelli
