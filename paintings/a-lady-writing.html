About this painting

Johannes Vermeer made A Lady Writing around 1665. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the National Gallery of Art, Washington.

Only about 35 paintings by Vermeer survive. The yellow jacket trimmed with ermine shows up in several of his other pictures.

About the artist

Vermeer spent his whole life in Delft and left only about 35 paintings. His quiet interiors, lit by soft daylight from a window, were nearly forgotten for two centuries before being rediscovered in the 1860s. More about Johannes Vermeer
