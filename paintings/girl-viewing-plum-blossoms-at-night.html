About this print

Suzuki Harunobu made Girl Viewing Plum Blossoms at Night around 1766. It is color woodblock print, and you can see it today at The Metropolitan Museum of Art.

Harunobu helped invent full-color woodblock printing in the 1760s. The solid black night sky makes the white blossoms and the girl’s lantern stand out.

About the artist

Harunobu helped pioneer full-color woodblock printing in the 1760s. His delicate, doll-like figures of young women and lovers made him the most popular print designer of his day. More about Suzuki Harunobu
