About this painting

Edvard Munch made The Scream in 1895. It is lithograph, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Munch made several versions of The Scream in paint, pastel and print. This black-and-white lithograph let him sell it to many more people.

About the artist

Munch was a Norwegian painter and printmaker whose works express anxiety, love and loss. The Scream, which he made in several versions, has become a universal image of modern angst. More about Edvard Munch
