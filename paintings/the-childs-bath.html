About this painting

Mary Cassatt made The Child’s Bath in 1893. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Cassatt was the only American to exhibit with the Impressionists in Paris. The high viewpoint and flat patterns come from Japanese prints.

About the artist

Cassatt was an American who settled in Paris and became the only American to exhibit with the Impressionists. She is best known for tender scenes of mothers and children. More about Mary Cassatt
