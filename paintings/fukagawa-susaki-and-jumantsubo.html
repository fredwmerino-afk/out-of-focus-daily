About this print

Utagawa Hiroshige made Fukagawa Susaki and Jūmantsubo in 1857. It is color woodblock print, and you can see it today at The Metropolitan Museum of Art.

We look down through the eyes of an eagle swooping over the snowy marshland at the edge of Edo Bay, with Mount Fuji on the horizon.

About the artist

Hiroshige was the great rival of Hokusai and the master of the travel print. His series Fifty-three Stations of the Tōkaidō and One Hundred Famous Views of Edo capture rain, snow, mist and moonlight, and Van Gogh copied two of his prints in oil. More about Utagawa Hiroshige
