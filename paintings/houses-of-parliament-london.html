About this painting

Claude Monet made Houses of Parliament, London in 1900–01. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Monet painted Parliament from a terrace of St Thomas’ Hospital across the Thames, often as the sun set behind it.

About the artist

Monet was a founder of Impressionism; one of his paintings, Impression, Sunrise, gave the movement its name. He painted outdoors to capture changing light, and in later life returned again and again to the same subjects, from haystacks to the water lily pond in his garden at Giverny.
