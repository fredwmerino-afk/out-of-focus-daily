About this painting

Claude Monet made Rocks at Port-Goulphar, Belle-Île in 1886. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Monet worked through Atlantic storms on Belle-Île, a rugged island off the coast of Brittany.

About the artist

Monet was a founder of Impressionism; one of his paintings, Impression, Sunrise, gave the movement its name. He painted outdoors to capture changing light, and in later life returned again and again to the same subjects, from haystacks to the water lily pond in his garden at Giverny.
