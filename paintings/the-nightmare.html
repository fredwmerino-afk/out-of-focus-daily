About this painting

Henry Fuseli made The Nightmare in 1781. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the Detroit Institute of Arts.

A sensation at the Royal Academy in 1782: a demon squats on a sleeping woman while a ghostly horse peers through the curtains.
