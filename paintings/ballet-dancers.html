About this painting

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec made Ballet Dancers (Toulouse-Lautrec) in 1885–86. It is oil on plaster, transferred to canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Painted directly on the wall of a country inn near Paris, then moved to canvas years later.

About the artist

Toulouse-Lautrec, from an aristocratic family, made the cabarets, dance halls and theaters of Montmartre his subject. His bold posters for the Moulin Rouge helped turn advertising into an art form. More about Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec
