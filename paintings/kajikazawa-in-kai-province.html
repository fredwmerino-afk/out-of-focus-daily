About this print

Katsushika Hokusai made Kajikazawa in Kai Province around 1830–32. It is color woodblock print, and you can see it today at The Metropolitan Museum of Art.

A fisherman braces on a rocky point above the churning river, his lines fanning out like the slopes of Fuji behind him. Early impressions were printed almost entirely in shades of blue.

About the artist

Hokusai made thousands of prints, paintings and drawings over a long career, using dozens of names. His series Thirty-Six Views of Mount Fuji, which includes The Great Wave, influenced Monet, Van Gogh and many other Western artists. More about Katsushika Hokusai
