About this painting

Eugène Delacroix made Women of Algiers in Their Apartment in 1834. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the Musée du Louvre, Paris.

Inspired by Delacroix’s 1832 trip to North Africa. More than a century later, Picasso painted 15 variations on it.

About the artist

Delacroix was the leading French Romantic painter, known for his vivid color and dramatic, energetic scenes of history, literature and North Africa. More about Eugène Delacroix
