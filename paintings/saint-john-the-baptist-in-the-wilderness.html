About this painting

Diego Velázquez made Saint John the Baptist in the Wilderness around 1622. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Long credited to an unknown Seville painter, it is now recognized as the work of the young Velázquez.

About the artist

Velázquez was court painter to King Philip IV of Spain for nearly forty years. His loose, confident brushwork and honest portraits, above all Las Meninas, made him a hero to later painters such as Manet. More about Diego Velázquez
