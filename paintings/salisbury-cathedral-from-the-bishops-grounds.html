About this painting

John Constable made Salisbury Cathedral from the Bishop’s Grounds around 1825. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York.

The bishop who commissioned the first version disliked its dark clouds, so Constable later painted one with a brighter sky.

About the artist

Constable painted the English countryside, especially the valley of the River Stour in Suffolk where he grew up. His fresh, direct studies of clouds and weather influenced French landscape painters. More about John Constable
