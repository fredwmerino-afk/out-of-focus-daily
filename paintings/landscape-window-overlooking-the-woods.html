About this painting

Édouard Vuillard made Landscape: Window Overlooking the Woods in 1899. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

A large decorative panel painted for a Paris apartment, meant to feel like looking out a window onto the woods.
