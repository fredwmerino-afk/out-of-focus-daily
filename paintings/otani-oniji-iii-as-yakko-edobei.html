About this print

Tōshūsai Sharaku made Ōtani Oniji III as Yakko Edobei in 1794. It is color woodblock print, and you can see it today at The Metropolitan Museum of Art.

The kabuki actor plays a villain about to grab money from a passerby. Sharaku made about 140 actor prints in roughly ten months, then vanished.

About the artist

Sharaku is one of the great mysteries of Japanese art. He produced about 140 striking prints of kabuki actors in about ten months in 1794–95, then vanished, and no one knows for sure who he was. More about Tōshūsai Sharaku
