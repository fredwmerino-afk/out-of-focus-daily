About this painting

Leonardo da Vinci made Ginevra de’ Benci around 1474–78. It is oil on panel, and you can see it today at the National Gallery of Art, Washington.

The only painting by Leonardo in the Americas. On the back is a juniper sprig, ginepro in Italian, a pun on her name, with the motto “Beauty adorns virtue.”

About the artist

Leonardo was a painter, engineer, scientist and inventor, the very model of a “Renaissance man.” Fewer than twenty paintings by him survive, among them the Mona Lisa and The Last Supper. More about Leonardo da Vinci
