About this painting

Edgar Degas made Yellow Dancers (In the Wings) in 1874–76. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Ballet was Degas’s favorite subject. He was fascinated by what happened backstage as much as by the performance.

About the artist

Degas exhibited with the Impressionists but preferred to call himself a realist. He is best known for his ballet dancers, painted from unusual angles, often working in pastel. More about Edgar Degas
