About this painting

Paul Gauguin made Te raau rahi (The Big Tree) in 1891. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

One of the first paintings Gauguin made after arriving in Tahiti in 1891.

About the artist

Gauguin left a career as a stockbroker to become a painter, and later left Europe altogether for Tahiti. His flat areas of bold color and dreamlike scenes of island life deeply influenced twentieth-century art. More about Paul Gauguin
