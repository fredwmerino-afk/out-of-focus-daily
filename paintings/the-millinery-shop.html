About this painting

Edgar Degas made The Millinery Shop in 1879–86. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Degas made a whole series of Paris hat-shop scenes. Here the hats get more attention than the woman.

About the artist

Degas exhibited with the Impressionists but preferred to call himself a realist. He is best known for his ballet dancers, painted from unusual angles, often working in pastel. More about Edgar Degas
