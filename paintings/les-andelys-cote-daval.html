About this painting

Paul Signac made Les Andelys, Côte d’Aval in 1886. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Painted in 1886, the year Signac and Seurat showed their new dot technique at the last Impressionist exhibition.
