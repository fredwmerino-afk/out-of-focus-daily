About this painting

Francisco de Zurbarán made The Crucifixion in 1627. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

In the dim monastery sacristy where it first hung, Zurbarán’s lifelike Christ was often mistaken for a sculpture.
