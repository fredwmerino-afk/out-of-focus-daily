About this painting

The artist made Virgin and Child (Rogier van der Weyden workshop) in 1460–1500. It is oil on panel, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

The cushion seems to rest on the edge of the frame, as if Mary were showing her child to us through a window.

The style: Renaissance
