About this painting

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec made Jane Avril in 1899. It is color lithograph (poster), and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Dancer Jane Avril was a friend and favorite model. This poster, with a snake coiling around her dress, was never used for publicity.

About the artist

Toulouse-Lautrec, from an aristocratic family, made the cabarets, dance halls and theaters of Montmartre his subject. His bold posters for the Moulin Rouge helped turn advertising into an art form. More about Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec
