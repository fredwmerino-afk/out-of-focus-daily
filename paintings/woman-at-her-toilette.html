About this painting

Berthe Morisot made Woman at Her Toilette in 1875–80. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Morisot showed her work in seven of the eight Impressionist exhibitions, more than almost anyone. She was married to Manet’s brother Eugène.
