About this painting

Camille Pissarro made The Place du Havre, Paris in 1893. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

An eye infection kept Pissarro indoors in his later years, so he painted busy Paris streets from hotel windows.

About the artist

Pissarro was the only artist to show work at all eight Impressionist exhibitions, and a generous mentor to younger painters, including Cézanne and Gauguin. He loved painting rural life and, later, the busy streets of Paris. More about Camille Pissarro
