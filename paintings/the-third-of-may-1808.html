About this painting

Francisco Goya made The Third of May 1808 in 1814. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the Museo del Prado, Madrid.

French soldiers execute Spanish civilians after an uprising against Napoleon’s army. The man in white raises his arms like a figure on a cross.

About the artist

Goya was court painter to the Spanish kings, but he is also remembered for his searing images of war and his dark, private late works. More about Francisco Goya
