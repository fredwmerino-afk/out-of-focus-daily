About this print

Kitagawa Utamaro made Three Beauties of the Present Day around 1792–93. It is color woodblock print, and you can see it today at The Metropolitan Museum of Art.

The three women were celebrities of 1790s Edo: a geisha and two teahouse waitresses. Each is identified by the family crest on her robe.

About the artist

Utamaro was the leading designer of prints of beautiful women in the 1790s, famous for his close-up “large head” portraits set against shimmering mica backgrounds. More about Kitagawa Utamaro
