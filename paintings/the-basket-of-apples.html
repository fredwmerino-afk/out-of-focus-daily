About this painting

Paul Cézanne made The Basket of Apples around 1893. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

The table edges don’t line up and the bottle leans. Cézanne bent the rules of perspective on purpose, which made this a key picture for Cubism.

About the artist

Cézanne worked largely alone in his native Provence, building his pictures from patches of color and simplified shapes. Picasso and Matisse both called him the father of modern art. More about Paul Cézanne
