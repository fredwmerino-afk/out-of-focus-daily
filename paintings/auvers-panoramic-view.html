About this painting

Paul Cézanne made Auvers, Panoramic View in 1873–75. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Cézanne painted this while working beside Camille Pissarro, who taught him to paint outdoors.

About the artist

Cézanne worked largely alone in his native Provence, building his pictures from patches of color and simplified shapes. Picasso and Matisse both called him the father of modern art. More about Paul Cézanne
