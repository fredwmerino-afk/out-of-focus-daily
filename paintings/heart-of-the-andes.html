About this painting

Frederic Edwin Church made Heart of the Andes in 1859. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York.

First shown alone in a darkened New York room, lit by gas lamps. Thousands paid a quarter to see it, some using opera glasses to study the details.
