About this painting

Paul Cézanne made The Plate of Apples around 1877. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Apples were Cézanne’s favorite subject. He said he wanted to astonish Paris with an apple.

About the artist

Cézanne worked largely alone in his native Provence, building his pictures from patches of color and simplified shapes. Picasso and Matisse both called him the father of modern art. More about Paul Cézanne
