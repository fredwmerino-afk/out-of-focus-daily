About this painting

Giovanni Battista Tiepolo made Armida Abandoned by Rinaldo in 1742–45. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

From the same Tasso series: the knight Rinaldo leaves the sorceress Armida to return to the Crusade.

About the artist

Tiepolo was the great Venetian decorator of the 1700s, painting luminous, sky-filled ceilings for palaces and churches across Italy, Germany and Spain. More about Giovanni Battista Tiepolo
