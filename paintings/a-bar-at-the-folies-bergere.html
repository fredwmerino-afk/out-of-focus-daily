About this painting

Édouard Manet made A Bar at the Folies-Bergère in 1882. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Courtauld, London.

Manet’s last major painting. Look at the mirror behind the barmaid: her reflection doesn’t line up with where she stands.

About the artist

Manet painted modern Parisian life in a bold, direct style that scandalized the art establishment. Though he never exhibited with the Impressionists, the younger painters saw him as their leader. More about Édouard Manet
