About this painting

Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec made Equestrienne (At the Cirque Fernando) in 1887–88. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

The owner of the Moulin Rouge bought this circus scene to hang in the club’s foyer when it opened in 1889.

About the artist

Toulouse-Lautrec, from an aristocratic family, made the cabarets, dance halls and theaters of Montmartre his subject. His bold posters for the Moulin Rouge helped turn advertising into an art form. More about Henri de Toulouse-Lautrec
