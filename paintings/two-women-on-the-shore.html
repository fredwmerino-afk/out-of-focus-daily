About this painting

Edvard Munch made Two Women on the Shore in 1898. It is color woodcut, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

A young woman in white and an old woman in black: Munch often paired youth and death on the shoreline near his summer house.

About the artist

Munch was a Norwegian painter and printmaker whose works express anxiety, love and loss. The Scream, which he made in several versions, has become a universal image of modern angst. More about Edvard Munch
