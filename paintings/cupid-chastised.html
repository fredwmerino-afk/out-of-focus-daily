About this painting

Bartolomeo Manfredi made Cupid Chastised in 1613. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Mars punishes Cupid as Venus tries to stop him. Manfredi was one of the leading followers of Caravaggio.
