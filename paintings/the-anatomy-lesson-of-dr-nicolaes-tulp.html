About this painting

Rembrandt van Rijn made The Anatomy Lesson of Dr. Nicolaes Tulp in 1632. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the Mauritshuis, The Hague.

Rembrandt was 26 and new to Amsterdam. The body is that of an executed thief, dissected at a public anatomy lesson.

About the artist

Rembrandt was the greatest painter of the Dutch Golden Age, celebrated for his group portraits, Bible scenes and dramatic use of light and shadow. His many self-portraits trace his life from confident young success to bankruptcy and old age. More about Rembrandt van Rijn
