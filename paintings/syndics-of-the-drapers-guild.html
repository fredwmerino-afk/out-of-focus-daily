About this painting

Rembrandt van Rijn made Syndics of the Drapers’ Guild in 1662. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam.

These officials checked the quality of dyed cloth in Amsterdam. Their portrait later became the logo of Dutch Masters cigars.

About the artist

Rembrandt was the greatest painter of the Dutch Golden Age, celebrated for his group portraits, Bible scenes and dramatic use of light and shadow. His many self-portraits trace his life from confident young success to bankruptcy and old age. More about Rembrandt van Rijn
