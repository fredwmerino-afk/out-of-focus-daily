About this painting

Giovanni Battista Tiepolo made Rinaldo and Armida in Her Garden in 1742–45. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

One of a set of scenes from Torquato Tasso’s epic poem Jerusalem Delivered, painted to decorate a room in a Venetian palace.

About the artist

Tiepolo was the great Venetian decorator of the 1700s, painting luminous, sky-filled ceilings for palaces and churches across Italy, Germany and Spain. More about Giovanni Battista Tiepolo
