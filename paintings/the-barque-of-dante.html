About this painting

Eugène Delacroix made The Barque of Dante in 1822. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the Musée du Louvre, Paris.

Delacroix’s first painting at the Salon, made at age 24. Dante and the poet Virgil cross a river in Hell as the damned cling to their boat.

About the artist

Delacroix was the leading French Romantic painter, known for his vivid color and dramatic, energetic scenes of history, literature and North Africa. More about Eugène Delacroix
