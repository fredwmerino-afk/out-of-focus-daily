About this painting

John Constable made The Hay Wain in 1821. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the National Gallery, London.

Barely noticed in London at first, it won a gold medal at the Paris Salon of 1824 and inspired a generation of French landscape painters.

About the artist

Constable painted the English countryside, especially the valley of the River Stour in Suffolk where he grew up. His fresh, direct studies of clouds and weather influenced French landscape painters. More about John Constable
