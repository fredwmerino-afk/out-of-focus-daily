About this painting

Jusepe de Ribera made Penitent Saint Peter in 1628–32. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Ribera spent his career in Naples, then ruled by Spain, where he was nicknamed “Lo Spagnoletto,” the little Spaniard.
