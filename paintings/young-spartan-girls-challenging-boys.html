About this painting

Edgar Degas made Young Spartan Girls Challenging Boys around 1860. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

An early history painting from before Degas turned to dancers. Another version hangs in the National Gallery, London.

About the artist

Degas exhibited with the Impressionists but preferred to call himself a realist. He is best known for his ballet dancers, painted from unusual angles, often working in pastel. More about Edgar Degas
