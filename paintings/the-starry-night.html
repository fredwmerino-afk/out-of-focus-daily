About this painting

Vincent van Gogh made The Starry Night in 1889. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the Museum of Modern Art, New York.

Painted at the asylum in Saint-Rémy from the view out his window, but the village and church were added from memory and imagination.

About the artist

Van Gogh painted for only about ten years, producing more than 800 paintings, yet sold very few in his lifetime. His thick, swirling brushwork and intense color, developed in Paris and the south of France, made him one of the most influential artists of all time. More about Vincent van Gogh
