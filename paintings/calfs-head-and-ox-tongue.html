About this painting

Gustave Caillebotte made Calf’s Head and Ox Tongue around 1882. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Caillebotte was a surprising figure: painter, wealthy collector, yacht designer and stamp collector. Here he paints a butcher’s display.

About the artist

Caillebotte was both a painter and a wealthy patron of the Impressionists, buying his friends’ works and leaving them to the French state. His own paintings show modern Paris from striking angles. More about Gustave Caillebotte
