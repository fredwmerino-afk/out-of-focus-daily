About this print

Katsushika Hokusai made The Great Wave off Kanagawa in 1830–33. It is color woodblock print, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

From the series Thirty-Six Views of Mount Fuji. Fuji is the small snowy peak framed by the wave.

About the artist

Hokusai made thousands of prints, paintings and drawings over a long career, using dozens of names. His series Thirty-Six Views of Mount Fuji, which includes The Great Wave, influenced Monet, Van Gogh and many other Western artists. More about Katsushika Hokusai
