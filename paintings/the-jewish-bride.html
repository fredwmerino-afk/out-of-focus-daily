About this painting

Rembrandt van Rijn made The Jewish Bride around 1665–69. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam.

Van Gogh said he would give ten years of his life to sit in front of it for two weeks with only a crust of bread. The title is a later nickname; who the couple are is unknown.

About the artist

Rembrandt was the greatest painter of the Dutch Golden Age, celebrated for his group portraits, Bible scenes and dramatic use of light and shadow. His many self-portraits trace his life from confident young success to bankruptcy and old age. More about Rembrandt van Rijn
