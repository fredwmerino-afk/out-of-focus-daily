About this painting

Henri-Edmond Cross made Beach at Cabasson (Baigne-Cul) in 1891–92. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Cross painted the Mediterranean coast in Seurat’s dot technique. Baigne-Cul is a cheeky local name for the beach.
