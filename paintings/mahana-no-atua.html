About this painting

Paul Gauguin made Mahana no atua (Day of the God) in 1894. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Gauguin painted this Tahitian scene in Paris, from memory and imagination. The bands of colored water at the bottom are pure invention.

About the artist

Gauguin left a career as a stockbroker to become a painter, and later left Europe altogether for Tahiti. His flat areas of bold color and dreamlike scenes of island life deeply influenced twentieth-century art. More about Paul Gauguin
