About this painting

Judith Leyster made Self-Portrait (Judith Leyster) around 1630. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the National Gallery of Art, Washington.

Leyster was one of the few women in Haarlem’s painters’ guild. For centuries many of her paintings were credited to Frans Hals.
