About this print

Katsushika Hokusai made Thunderstorm Beneath the Summit (Black Fuji) around 1830–32. It is color woodblock print, and you can see it today at The Metropolitan Museum of Art.

Lightning strikes the dark lower slopes while the peak rises into clear sky. It mirrors the composition of Red Fuji, and the two are often shown side by side.

About the artist

Hokusai made thousands of prints, paintings and drawings over a long career, using dozens of names. His series Thirty-Six Views of Mount Fuji, which includes The Great Wave, influenced Monet, Van Gogh and many other Western artists. More about Katsushika Hokusai
