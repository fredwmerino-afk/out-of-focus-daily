About this painting

Henri Fantin-Latour made Édouard Manet in 1867. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Fantin-Latour and Manet were close friends. A few years later he painted Manet again, at his easel and surrounded by younger artists, in A Studio at Les Batignolles.
