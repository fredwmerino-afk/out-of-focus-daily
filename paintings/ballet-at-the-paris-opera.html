About this painting

Edgar Degas made Ballet at the Paris Opéra in 1877. It is pastel over monotype on paper, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Degas often drew in pastel on top of a monotype print. The view is from the orchestra seats, with musicians’ instruments in the foreground.

About the artist

Degas exhibited with the Impressionists but preferred to call himself a realist. He is best known for his ballet dancers, painted from unusual angles, often working in pastel. More about Edgar Degas
