About this painting

Johannes Vermeer made View of Delft around 1660–61. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the Mauritshuis, The Hague.

The French novelist Marcel Proust called it the most beautiful painting in the world.

About the artist

Vermeer spent his whole life in Delft and left only about 35 paintings. His quiet interiors, lit by soft daylight from a window, were nearly forgotten for two centuries before being rediscovered in the 1860s. More about Johannes Vermeer
