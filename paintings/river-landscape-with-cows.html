About this painting

Aelbert Cuyp made River Landscape with Cows in 1645–50. It is oil on panel, and you can see it today at the National Gallery of Art, Washington.

Cuyp painted from a low viewpoint so the cows, bathed in late-afternoon light, take on a quiet grandeur.
