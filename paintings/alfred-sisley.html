About this painting

Pierre-Auguste Renoir made Alfred Sisley in 1876. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Renoir and Sisley met as art students in Paris in the early 1860s, along with Monet. This is Renoir’s portrait of his friend.

About the artist

Renoir was a leading Impressionist, known for warm, glowing scenes of people enjoying themselves: dances, boating parties, cafés and family life. Late in life, despite severe arthritis, he kept on painting. More about Pierre-Auguste Renoir
