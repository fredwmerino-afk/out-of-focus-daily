About this painting

El Greco made Saint Martin and the Beggar around 1597–1600. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Martin wears fashionable armor of El Greco’s own day, and the walls of Toledo appear in the distance. A larger version hangs in Washington.

About the artist

Born in Crete, El Greco (“the Greek”) trained in Venice and Rome before settling in Toledo, Spain. His elongated figures and flickering colors looked strange to many of his contemporaries but inspired modern artists centuries later. More about El Greco
