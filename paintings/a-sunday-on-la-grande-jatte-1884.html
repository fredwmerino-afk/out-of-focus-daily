About this painting

Georges Seurat made A Sunday on La Grande Jatte — 1884 in 1884–86. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Seurat built the whole picture from tiny dots of unmixed color, a technique called pointillism. It took him about two years.

About the artist

Seurat invented pointillism, building pictures from tiny dots of pure color that blend in the viewer’s eye. He died at just 31, having completed only a handful of large paintings. More about Georges Seurat
