About this painting

Frans Snyders made Still Life with Dead Game, Fruits, and Vegetables in a Market in 1614. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Snyders often painted the animals and food in Rubens’s pictures. Here a market stall overflows with game.
