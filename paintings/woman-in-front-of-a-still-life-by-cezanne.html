About this painting

Paul Gauguin made Woman in Front of a Still Life by Cézanne in 1890. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Gauguin owned a Cézanne still life and placed it behind this sitter as a tribute to the older painter.

About the artist

Gauguin left a career as a stockbroker to become a painter, and later left Europe altogether for Tahiti. His flat areas of bold color and dreamlike scenes of island life deeply influenced twentieth-century art. More about Paul Gauguin
