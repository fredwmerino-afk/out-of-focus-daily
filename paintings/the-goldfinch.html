About this painting

Carel Fabritius made The Goldfinch in 1654. It is oil on panel, and you can see it today at the Mauritshuis, The Hague.

Painted the year Fabritius died, aged 32, in the explosion of a gunpowder store in Delft. It inspired Donna Tartt’s novel The Goldfinch.
