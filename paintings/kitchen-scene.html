About this painting

Diego Velázquez made Kitchen Scene in 1618–20. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

A bodegón, or kitchen picture, from Velázquez’s youth in Seville. The young African woman at work may have been modeled by an enslaved servant.

About the artist

Velázquez was court painter to King Philip IV of Spain for nearly forty years. His loose, confident brushwork and honest portraits, above all Las Meninas, made him a hero to later painters such as Manet. More about Diego Velázquez
