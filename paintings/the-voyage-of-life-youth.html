About this painting

Thomas Cole made The Voyage of Life: Youth in 1842. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the National Gallery of Art, Washington.

One of four paintings following a life from childhood to old age. Here the youth steers eagerly toward a castle in the sky.

About the artist

Cole is considered the founder of the Hudson River School, the first American landscape movement, celebrating the grandeur of the American wilderness. More about Thomas Cole
