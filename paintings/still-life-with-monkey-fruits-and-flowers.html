About this painting

Jean-Baptiste Oudry made Still Life with Monkey, Fruits, and Flowers in 1724. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Oudry later ran the Beauvais tapestry works and was famous for painting the king’s hunting dogs.
