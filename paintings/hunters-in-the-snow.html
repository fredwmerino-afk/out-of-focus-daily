About this painting

Pieter Bruegel the Elder made Hunters in the Snow in 1565. It is oil on oak panel, and you can see it today at the Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna.

One of a series Bruegel painted on the seasons of the year for a wealthy Antwerp merchant. Five of them survive.

About the artist

Bruegel painted Flemish peasants, landscapes and proverbs with humor and sharp observation. His sons and grandsons continued the family tradition of painting. More about Pieter Bruegel the Elder
