About this painting

Pierre-Auguste Renoir made Two Sisters (On the Terrace) in 1881. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Painted on the terrace of the Restaurant Fournaise at Chatou. The two models weren’t actually sisters.

About the artist

Renoir was a leading Impressionist, known for warm, glowing scenes of people enjoying themselves: dances, boating parties, cafés and family life. Late in life, despite severe arthritis, he kept on painting. More about Pierre-Auguste Renoir
