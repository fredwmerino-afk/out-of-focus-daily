About this painting

Filippino Lippi made The Coronation of the Virgin around 1475. It is tempera and oil on poplar panel, and you can see it today at the National Gallery of Art, Washington.

One of Filippino’s earliest works. He was the son of the painter Fra Filippo Lippi and trained with Botticelli.
