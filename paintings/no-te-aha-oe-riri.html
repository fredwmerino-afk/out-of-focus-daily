About this painting

Paul Gauguin made No te aha oe riri (Why Are You Angry?) in 1896. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Made on Gauguin’s second and final trip to Tahiti, where he lived for the rest of his life.

About the artist

Gauguin left a career as a stockbroker to become a painter, and later left Europe altogether for Tahiti. His flat areas of bold color and dreamlike scenes of island life deeply influenced twentieth-century art. More about Paul Gauguin
