About this painting

Gustav Klimt made The Kiss in 1907–08. It is oil and gold leaf on canvas, and you can see it today at the Belvedere, Vienna.

Klimt’s father was a gold engraver, and real gold leaf covers much of the canvas. The Austrian state bought it the year it was first shown.

About the artist

Klimt was a founder of the Vienna Secession, an artists’ group that broke with the academic establishment. His “golden period” paintings use real gold leaf and rich patterns. More about Gustav Klimt
