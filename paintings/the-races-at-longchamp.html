About this painting

Édouard Manet made The Races at Longchamp in 1866. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Manet put the horses charging straight at the viewer, a startling choice when racing pictures usually showed them side-on.

About the artist

Manet painted modern Parisian life in a bold, direct style that scandalized the art establishment. Though he never exhibited with the Impressionists, the younger painters saw him as their leader. More about Édouard Manet
