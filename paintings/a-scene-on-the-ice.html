About this painting

Hendrick Avercamp made A Scene on the Ice around 1625. It is oil on panel, and you can see it today at the National Gallery of Art, Washington.

Nobles, farmers and fishermen share the frozen river. Avercamp filled his winter scenes with dozens of tiny stories.

About the artist

Avercamp, who could not speak and was known as “the Mute of Kampen,” specialized in winter scenes crowded with skaters during the Little Ice Age. More about Hendrick Avercamp
