About this painting

Jan van Eyck made The Arnolfini Portrait in 1434. It is oil on oak panel, and you can see it today at the National Gallery, London.

Above the mirror the artist wrote “Jan van Eyck was here 1434.” The mirror shows two more people standing in the doorway.

About the artist

Van Eyck worked in Bruges and perfected the technique of oil painting, achieving detail and luminous color that astonished his contemporaries. More about Jan van Eyck
