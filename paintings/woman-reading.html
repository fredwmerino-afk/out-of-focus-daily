About this painting

Édouard Manet made Woman Reading in 1880–82. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Painted near the end of Manet’s life, it shows a woman in a café holding up an illustrated journal on a stick.

About the artist

Manet painted modern Parisian life in a bold, direct style that scandalized the art establishment. Though he never exhibited with the Impressionists, the younger painters saw him as their leader. More about Édouard Manet
