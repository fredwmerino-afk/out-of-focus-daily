About this painting

Hieronymus Bosch made The Garden of Earthly Delights around 1490–1510. It is oil on oak panel, and you can see it today at the Museo del Prado, Madrid.

Read left to right: Eden, a crowded paradise of pleasures, then hell. Closed, the wings show the world as a glass globe.

About the artist

Bosch filled his paintings with fantastical creatures, strange machines and visions of heaven and hell. Very little is known about his life in the Dutch town of ’s-Hertogenbosch. More about Hieronymus Bosch
