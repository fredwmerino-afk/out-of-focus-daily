About this painting

Giovanni Battista Tiepolo made Virgin and Child with Saints Dominic and Hyacinth in 1730–35. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Tiepolo made the Virgin and Child seem to appear on the altar in a vision, wrapped in golden clouds.

About the artist

Tiepolo was the great Venetian decorator of the 1700s, painting luminous, sky-filled ceilings for palaces and churches across Italy, Germany and Spain. More about Giovanni Battista Tiepolo
