About this painting

Sandro Botticelli made Virgin and Child with an Angel in 1475–85. It is tempera on panel, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Botticelli learned his craft from the painter Fra Filippo Lippi. Here Mary presses her cheek to her son’s as an angel looks on.

About the artist

Botticelli was a favorite painter of Florence’s Medici family. He is best known for graceful mythological paintings like The Birth of Venus, as well as many tender Madonnas. More about Sandro Botticelli
