About this painting

Édouard Manet made Luncheon on the Grass (Le Déjeuner sur l’herbe) in 1863. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the Musée d’Orsay, Paris.

Rejected by the official Salon, it caused a scandal at the 1863 Salon des Refusés: a nude woman picnicking with two fully dressed modern men.

About the artist

Manet painted modern Parisian life in a bold, direct style that scandalized the art establishment. Though he never exhibited with the Impressionists, the younger painters saw him as their leader. More about Édouard Manet
