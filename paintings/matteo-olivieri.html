About this painting

The artist made Matteo Olivieri (?) in 1430s. It is tempera (and oil?) on panel, transferred to canvas, and you can see it today at the National Gallery of Art, Washington.

The gold inscription names the sitter, but nobody knows who painted him. Over the years it has been credited to Domenico Veneziano and to Paolo Uccello.
