About this painting

The artist made Francesco de’ Medici around 1560. It is oil on panel, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Painted fourteen years before Francesco became Grand Duke of Tuscany. He ruled from 1574 until his death in 1587.

The style: Renaissance
