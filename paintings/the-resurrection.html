About this painting

Cecco del Caravaggio made The Resurrection in 1619–20. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Cecco may have been the “boy Francesco” who assisted Caravaggio in his last years in Rome, hence his nickname.
