About this painting

Balthasar van der Ast made Basket of Flowers around 1622. It is oil on panel, and you can see it today at the National Gallery of Art, Washington.

Like most flower pieces of the time, it combines blooms from different seasons, so no real bouquet ever looked quite like this.
