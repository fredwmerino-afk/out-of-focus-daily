About this painting

Frans Hals made Portrait of a Woman Aged Sixty in 1633. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at the National Gallery of Art, Washington.

The inscription beside her head gives her age, 60, and the year, 1633. Who she was is no longer known.

About the artist

Hals worked in Haarlem and was famous for lively portraits painted with fast, loose brushstrokes, a technique admired two centuries later by Manet and Van Gogh. More about Frans Hals
