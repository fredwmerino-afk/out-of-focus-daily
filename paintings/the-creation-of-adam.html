About this painting

Michelangelo Buonarroti made The Creation of Adam around 1508–12. It is fresco, and you can see it today at the Sistine Chapel, Vatican City.

Part of the Sistine Chapel ceiling. Michelangelo painted it standing on scaffolding with his head tipped back, not lying down as legend says.

About the artist

Michelangelo was a sculptor first, carving the David before he was thirty, but he also painted the Sistine Chapel ceiling and the Last Judgment, and designed the dome of St. Peter’s in Rome. More about Michelangelo Buonarroti
