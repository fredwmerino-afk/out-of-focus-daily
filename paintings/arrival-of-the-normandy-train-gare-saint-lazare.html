About this painting

Claude Monet made Arrival of the Normandy Train, Gare Saint-Lazare in 1877. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

One of about a dozen paintings of the Saint-Lazare station. Legend says Monet had trains held so the station would fill with steam.

About the artist

Monet was a founder of Impressionism; one of his paintings, Impression, Sunrise, gave the movement its name. He painted outdoors to capture changing light, and in later life returned again and again to the same subjects, from haystacks to the water lily pond in his garden at Giverny.
