About this painting

Paul Gauguin made Merahi metua no Tehamana (Tehamana Has Many Parents) in 1893. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Tehamana was Gauguin’s Tahitian wife during his first stay. The carved figures and script behind her are Gauguin’s own inventions.

About the artist

Gauguin left a career as a stockbroker to become a painter, and later left Europe altogether for Tahiti. His flat areas of bold color and dreamlike scenes of island life deeply influenced twentieth-century art. More about Paul Gauguin
