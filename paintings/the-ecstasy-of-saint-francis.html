About this painting

Giovanni Baglione made The Ecstasy of Saint Francis in 1601. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Baglione borrowed heavily from Caravaggio, then became his bitter rival and sued him for libel in 1603.
