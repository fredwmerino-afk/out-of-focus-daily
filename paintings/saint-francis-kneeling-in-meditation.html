About this painting

El Greco made Saint Francis Kneeling in Meditation around 1595–1600. It is oil on canvas, and you can see it today at The Art Institute of Chicago.

Saint Francis in prayer was one of El Greco’s most popular subjects, and he and his workshop painted it many times.

About the artist

Born in Crete, El Greco (“the Greek”) trained in Venice and Rome before settling in Toledo, Spain. His elongated figures and flickering colors looked strange to many of his contemporaries but inspired modern artists centuries later. More about El Greco
